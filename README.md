# Echolyx AI

Acoustic predictive maintenance for industrial equipment.

## Live Demo

[Deployed on Vercel](https://echolyx.vercel.app)

## Overview

Echolyx listens to the sound of industrial equipment and flags abnormalities before they turn into downtime and repair bills. The platform uses acoustic sensors to monitor machines, learn their "healthy sound fingerprint," and detect anomalies in real time.

## Features

- **Weeks of early warning**: Catches bearing wear, imbalance, and cavitation before they show up on maintenance logs
- **No new hardware**: Lightweight acoustic sensors clip onto existing equipment — no rewiring, no downtime
- **87% reduction in unplanned downtime**: Proven across monitored fleets
- **One view for the whole floor**: Every monitored machine ranked by urgency
- **Learns your equipment**: Models tune to each machine's own healthy sound over time
